# Bar: trybooster.com (homepage, captured 2026-10-03)

Mechanisms observed on Booster's homepage. Each one is checkable from a screenshot.

1. **Hero headline is ~5x body size and stops at two lines.** 64px headline vs ~13px body, flush-left, 2 lines max. The hero has exactly two CTAs side by side directly under the copy.
2. **A phone number sits above the nav, before any scroll.** A utility strip with the phone number is visible in the first 40px of every viewport.
3. **Proof comes immediately after the hero.** The first thing below the fold is evidence (client logos), not a service explanation.
4. **One brand hue carries every section; backgrounds alternate dark → white → tint.** No two adjacent sections share a background. The dark brand colour is used for at most 1 in 3 sections.
5. **Explanatory sections are 50/50 text plus one large visual.** Each "how it works" or "about" block pairs a short text column with a single large media element (video, photo, illustration), and never more than one visual per block.
6. **Hard numbers as proof.** A stat block of 3 figures where the figure is ~3x the size of its label and set in the brand colour.
7. **Each section has three type sizes at most:** heading, one-line or two-line intro, body. The section heading is at least 2x body size.

## Where we must beat it

- Booster's photography is stock-like and purple-tinted. Ours is a **real fleet and real depot**, so we beat it on authenticity by never tinting.
- Booster's hero is text on a gradient. Ours is a **full-bleed photo of our own tanker**.
- We have no client logos. We cannot fabricate them, so proof after the hero has to be **real operational facts**: 24/7, depot tank capacity (23 000 L per tank, as shown on the tank signage) and the service area. Use only facts visible in our photos or the brief.
